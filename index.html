Not a shuffle. Each track is tagged to the same four moods as the shelf above — Comfort, Chaos, Heartbreak, Wonder — so whichever film you just watched hands off to a song built for the same feeling, instead of dropping you into something tonally random.

The idea, briefly

Most people already do this manually — a movie ends, and they open a different app to find a song that matches how it left them feeling. Entertainment Haven just makes that connection explicit: four moods run underneath both shelves, so "the queue" isn't a generic playlist, it's the soundtrack for whatever you picked to watch. The meaning of a playlist, in other words, comes from what it's in conversation with — not just what's in it.
